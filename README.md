# Experience Obliterator

<a href='https://neoforged.net/'><img alt="neoforge" height="56" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@intergrav/devins-badges@3/assets/cozy/supported/neoforge_vector.svg"></a>

Experience Obliterator is an extremely simple mod allowing you to disable or hide all of Minecraft's experience-related
mechanics!

## Features

The mod offers many configurable tweaks that help completely **obliterate** experience:

* Disable the spawning of experience orbs entirely or simply disable their rendering to make
  them invisible.
* Immediately pickup experience orbs when they spawn.
* Hide the experience bar from your HUD.
* Disable experience-related displays from [EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi) when loaded.
* Mute all sounds associated with picking up experience orbs and gaining levels.
* Disable the functionality of Anvils and Enchanting Tables.
* Support for removing Experience-related tooltips from [Echo Chest](https://modrinth.com/mod/echo-chest).
